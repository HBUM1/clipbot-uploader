Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

ClipBot Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What the app uses

When the owner connects their TikTok or YouTube account, the app receives a login token and basic profile info (such as account name) from that platform. These are used only to upload videos the owner picks to the owner's own account.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent anywhere except back to TikTok or YouTube to make uploads.

Other people

The app does not collect data from anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in their TikTok or Google account settings. Deleting the app's files deletes all stored tokens.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
